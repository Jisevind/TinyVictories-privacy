# Tiny Victories — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Tiny Victories ("the app") is an Android app for logging small daily accomplishments. It is built to keep your data on your own device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-in.
- No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- The app has no internet permission. It cannot send your data to me or anyone else.
- Your victories and settings are stored only on your phone.

## What the app stores

Everything below stays on your device in the app's private storage:

- The victories you log (text, date and time).
- Your settings: display name (optional), quick-add presets, theme, and reminder preferences.

I do not receive, see or have access to any of it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to show the gentle reminders you choose to turn on.
- **Receive boot completed** — to re-schedule your reminders after your phone restarts or the time zone or clock changes.

## How data can leave your device

Only through mechanisms you control or that Android provides, never through the app contacting a server:

1. **Android backup.** Your data may be included in your Google backup, but only when that backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. This is handled by Android and Google under their own privacy policies; the app has no access to it.
2. **Device-to-device transfer.** When you set up a new phone, Android can move the app's data across.
3. **Backup files.** "Save a backup file" in Profile writes a JSON file to a location you choose. What you do with that file is up to you.

## Deleting your data

- Use **Profile → Delete all data** to remove all victories.
- Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Backups you made yourself, or that Android made for you, are separate and managed by you and by Google.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, since it collects nothing.

## Not medical advice

Tiny Victories is a journaling and encouragement tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you are struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional or local support service.
